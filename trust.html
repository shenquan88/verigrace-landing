KYA Principles · v1

KYA — Know Your Agent. These principles govern how Verigrace operates agent-mediated payments and what we ask of every participant in our registries.

01 Disclosed Identity

Agents that transact through Verigrace register a declared identity — operator, endpoint, and capability claims — signed and queryable before money moves.

02 Settlement History Is Credit

Reputation is not self-attested. Payment success rates, fulfillment, and dispute records accumulate into a verifiable credit profile any buyer can check for a micro-fee.

03 Licensed Payment Rails

The payment core must remain with licensed, regulated infrastructure. Agents orchestrate; settlement settles on established rails (x402 over Base USDC), never in the dark.

04 Every Deal Has a Dispute Path

No silent failures. Funds in agent-to-agent work move through escrow with a defined verification and arbitration path — and graceful recovery over double charging.

05 Compliance Before Reach

Sanctions screening, cross-border data boundaries, and jurisdictional reachability are part of the product — declared per endpoint, not left to chance.

Alignment with the 2026 KYA industry covenant

These principles align with the industry self-regulatory covenant for agent payment applications published by the Payment & Clearing Association of China on 2026-08-24 — widely reported as the first KYA (Know Your Agent) rule of its kind — which calls for agent identity mechanisms, licensed-institution payment cores, and consumer protection. Verigrace endorses that direction and commits to operating by it across both of our operating jurisdictions.
